# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, considerate and professional.
- Assume good intent while being open to correction.
- Critique ideas and technical approaches, not people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's confidential information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks or deliberate humiliation.
- Trolling, insults, threats or sustained disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, proprietary code, confidential architecture or personal data.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional engineering community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository Issues, Discussions, Pull Requests, review comments and other project spaces where someone represents the project or its community.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or maintainers through their GitHub profile or another private contact method published in the repository. Do not create a public Issue containing sensitive allegations or personal information. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly, review relevant context and take proportionate action.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, request behaviour changes, restrict participation or remove access when needed to protect the community. Decisions should be fair, proportionate and explained when it is safe and appropriate to do so.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
